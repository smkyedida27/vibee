[Company legal name] ("we", "us") operates the Quintile mobile app (the "App"). This

## 1. What we collect

| Data | When | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| App-generated user ID and device identifier for guest play | first launch | keep your progress, streaks and leaderboard entries |
| Gameplay data (puzzles played, moves submitted, scores, times) | when you play daily challenges | validate scores, leaderboards, statistics |
| Display name, avatar, country (optional, chosen by you) | profile settings | leaderboards and friends |
| Email / account identifier from Google sign-in (optional) | if you link an account | restore progress across devices |
| Purchase records (product, order id, purchase token) | when you buy | deliver and restore purchases, prevent fraud |
| Push notification token | if you enable reminders | send the reminders you asked for |
| Crash logs and diagnostics | automatically | fix crashes (Firebase Crashlytics) |
| Usage analytics (screens, feature events) and advertising ID | automatically, subject to consent where required | improve the game; show and measure ads (Google AdMob) |

We do not collect contacts, precise location, photos, or microphone data. We do not
sell personal data.

## 2. Third parties

## 3. Advertising & consent

Where required (e.g. EEA, UK), we ask for consent before showing personalised ads. You
can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy. You can reset or delete your
advertising ID in Android settings.

## 4. Retention

Account and gameplay data is kept while your account is active. Leaderboard history is
kept for [24] months. Crash data is kept for [90] days. Purchase records are kept as long
as required by tax and accounting law.

## 5. Your rights & deletion

You can delete your account and associated data in the App (Settings → Delete account)
or at [https://puzzlehub.example/delete-account]. Deletion removes your profile, progress,
leaderboard entries and tokens within [30] days, except records we must keep by law
(e.g. purchase invoices). Depending on where you live you may also have rights to
access, correct, or port your data — contact us at [privacy@example.com].

## 6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access is restricted to authorised
personnel on a least-privilege basis.

## 7. Children
