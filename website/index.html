Questions

When does Quintile launch?

You can play the early-access Android version right now with the Download button. The Google Play and App Store versions are coming soon, and the badges on this page will link to them on launch day.

How do I install the Android download?

Tap Download now on your Android phone. When the file finishes, open it and allow installing apps from your browser if Android asks. Quintile appears on your home screen. When the Google Play version launches, you can switch to it for automatic updates.

Is it free?

Yes. Every puzzle and level is free to play. Optional extras like hints and an ad-free pass will be available in the app.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. All levels and the daily puzzle play offline. Leaderboards and syncing need a connection.

Which phones will it run on?

Android phones first, through Google Play, then iPhone through the App Store.
